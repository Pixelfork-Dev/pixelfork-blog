## Angle / thesis
Store listings convert better when people can try before installing (or at least see a real playable, not only trailer footage). A Pixelfork playable link gives instant browser play; a custom domain makes it brand-owned for campaigns. Use the web playable as the top of funnel; APK/AAB remain the Android artifacts. Don’t confuse a web demo with store presence.
