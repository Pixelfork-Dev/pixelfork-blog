Shipping is a skill of its own. Here is a practical path from finished build to a live store page.

## Start on itch.io

itch.io is the fastest way to get your game in front of players.

1. Create a project page and upload a zipped build or an HTML5 export.
2. Add at least **five screenshots**, a short GIF and a clear one-line pitch.
3. Choose a pricing model: free, paid, or pay-what-you-want.
4. Tag your game accurately. Tags drive most discovery on itch.io.

Use itch.io for game jam entries, demos and early feedback before a bigger launch.

## Moving to Steam

### Before you pay the fee

- Have a **capsule image** that reads at thumbnail size.
- Record a trailer that shows gameplay in the first five seconds.
- Write a store description that leads with what the player *does*.

### Wishlists are everything

Open your Steam page **months** before launch. Wishlists tell Steam's algorithm there is demand, and wishlisters get an email when you release.

### Launch checklist

- Test the build on a clean machine.
- Set a launch discount of 10–20%.
- Plan a demo for a Steam Next Fest.
- Line up content creators with keys a week early.

## After launch

Reply to reviews, ship a quick patch for any bugs players report, and share progress updates. Games that keep improving keep selling.
